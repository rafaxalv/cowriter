# Archived alternative: The first offer leads to rescue

This is the former official Purchase arc, archived when the new Albino-offer structure was selected (Nov 4, 2026). Chapter numbers below refer to the former plan; the current plan of record is in `outline/chapters.md`.

## Former sequence

1. **Old Ch. 10: first meeting and price.** During Benedito's years of poverty in Corumba, he works for Albino from time to time and meets Carmen at the tavern. Everyone thinks she is Albino's niece. She is afraid of Benedito at first. In later stolen conversations she tells him she was trafficked, is not free and is being sold; she gives him her price, one conto. Albino watches them and increasingly finds reasons to send Benedito away, especially because he will not sell her to a Black man.
2. **Old Ch. 11: Her Road before the win.** Over many meetings at Albino's, Carmen tells her story from Galicia through the crossing, Buenos Aires, the sale and the river journey to Corumba. Benedito hears the details before the lottery. He gives her a ticket; she says, "Men believe in luck; women count." Their last meeting is in April-May 1877, before the June draw; he buys a ticket with his last coins.
3. **Old Ch. 12: the win, threat and Bastian's arrival.** The draw is in Rio around June 1877; the result reaches Corumba in mid-July. On Day 1 Benedito offers one conto on a note against the prize. Albino refuses; Benedito loses his temper and threatens to expose the trafficking. Albino hides Carmen, writes to Madame Angelique and the claim letter goes missing; Benedito later suspects Albino asked his friend, the lottery agent, to hold it, but neither man admits it. Bastian arrives in late November with news from Bahia and Luzia's letters; the Tonico Interlude follows.
4. **Old Ch. 13: letters, race and rescue.** Benedito reads Tonico's letter and Luzia's bundle. Bastian remembers Alvarado boasting aboard their steamer that he has come to collect Carmen. Benedito realizes the boat will return in a few weeks and begins a race for money. The agent offers to buy his claim at a ruinous discount, nobody will lend, Firmino offers his savings, and Seu Bento discovers the claim letter was never sent. Benedito offers Albino three contos and silence; Albino refuses. Carmen recognizes Alvarado and lies about her virginity; Albino plans to send her downriver. Benedito and Bastian fight Albino's and Alvarado's men at the port, rescue Carmen and capture Alvarado alive. Bastian is wounded. Benedito uses Alvarado's testimony and the threat of police exposure to bargain: two contos when the prize arrives, a guardian's paper and silence. He buys Carmen and tells her she will be his wife; she has not chosen it. The prize later arrives and he pays Albino; they marry.

## What the former arc tested

The letters changed Benedito from waiting on luck to taking action. The rescue and the deal made the purchase a choice, but also repeated the ownership he thought he was undoing. The selected alternative moves the offer before Carmen's full story: Albino's offer and whitening argument help draw out Benedito's envy, and her story then reveals the life and fear behind the opportunity he thought she had.
