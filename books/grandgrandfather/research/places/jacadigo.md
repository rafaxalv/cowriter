# Place: Jacadigo (setting of the farm "Liberdade")

Research note, 7 Oct 2026. Where the protagonist's farms stand.

## What Jacadigo is
A rural area in the municipality of Corumbá (Mato Grosso do Sul), south and southwest of the town, on the edge of the Pantanal and against the Bolivian border. Not a town. The name is shared by three nearby features:

- **Serra / Morraria do Jacadigo (Jacadigo hill)**: about 19°11'34" S, 57°36'15" W (approximate). Roughly 20 km south of Corumbá. Part of the Urucum hills; a mountain of iron and manganese ore cut by the Brazil–Bolivia border (the Bolivian side is the Serranía del Mutún).
- **Lagoa do Jacadigo**: a lake at about 19°12'12" S, 57°48'10" W, elevation about 215 m. Roughly 25–30 km southwest of Corumbá, very close to the Bolivian border (Mutún is about 11 km west). It lies on a plain at the foot of the Urucum hills, more than 20 km from the Paraguay River floodplain, and is reached by river water only in years of great floods. Locals say it has shrunk in recent decades.
- **The Pantanal sub-region "Nabileque / Jacadigo"**: listed among Corumbá's Pantanal units.

Corumbá itself: 19°00'33" S, 57°39'12" W.

## What this means for the book
- From Corumbá, the farm is a short ride (a few hours on horseback): close enough for town business, far enough to feel isolated.
- Landscape: hills of iron-red rock behind, flat plain and lagoons in front, the Pantanal floods in big years, Bolivia just across the hills.
- The border matters: fugitives and smugglers, Bolivian neighbours and workers.
