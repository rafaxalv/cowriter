# Bastião

- Speech voice: [`CHAR-BASTIAO-SPEECH`](../voices/CHAR-BASTIAO-SPEECH.md)
- Part of the Character Bible; the index, the stages and the Continuity record are in `characters/character-bible.md`.

  - **Bastião**, best friend, two or three years older, enslaved on the estate; Velho Kalunga's student.
      - Knew Benedito as a boy but not closely; teased him, jokingly, as the master's son's pet (ch. 2).
      - Sees his anger when Luzia is sold and reaches out; brings him into the senzala's secret world, the roda, the terreiro, Kalunga (ch. 3).
      - Calls him "the milk brother" as a tease (ch. 2); when Benedito joins the Kalunga clan: "Does this make us the coffee brothers?" (ch. 3). The coffee brothers are only the two of them; Tonico's letter closes the joke: he signs as the milk brother, never a coffee brother (ch. 13).
      - At first teases him in the roda for not knowing its traditions; the teasing turns into friendship, and he stands by him against Ezequiel. With Kalunga, Benedito's first family after Luzia (ch. 3).
      - Capoeirista, joker, storyteller.
      - Real aspect: capoeira as resistance; quilombos in the Recôncavo.
      - Smart, a very good fighter, taught by Kalunga; knows his way around.
      - Killed in the 1890s defending Liberdade when the Pires' men steal its cattle; Benedito buries him and cannot prove who did it (ch. 17).
      - Chapters: ch. 2–3; refuses to go to the war and runs to a quilombo (ch. 3); kills Ezequiel when he burns the hideout and kills Kalunga, c. 1868; then lives alone (Interlude); cannot read: Tonico brings him the newspaper and a pass, and he crosses Brazil carrying Luzia's letters and Tonico's, told to hand over Tonico's first (Interlude), arriving in Corumbá c. late November 1877, four or five months after the win (ch. 12); stands with him when he challenges Albino for the woman from Spain (ch. 13); one of the most trusted men at Liberdade (ch. 14); dies before him, and Benedito buries him (ch. 17).
