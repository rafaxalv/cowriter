# Tonico (Antônio de Menezes)

- Speech voice: [`CHAR-TONICO-SPEECH`](../voices/CHAR-TONICO-SPEECH.md)
- Part of the Character Bible; the index, the stages and the Continuity record are in `characters/character-bible.md`.

  - **Antônio (Tonico) de Menezes**, the master's son, his age; his milk brother.
      - Weak and charming. True friends as small boys; estranged after Luzia's sale: not enemies, not friends.
      - Takes on the estate as he grows, working with his father, rarely around; Ezequiel defies him, and Benedito blames him for doing nothing (ch. 3).
      - Never a candidate for the war; watches his milk brother leave and stays. A loyal friend after all.
      - Real aspect: the exemption of the elite; the "milk brother" bond.
      - Chapters: ch. 1–2, 3; fires Ezequiel after finding in the accounts how well his father paid him (c. 1868), goes looking for Luzia out of guilt, finds her and brings her back to Cachoeira as a paid nurse, near the estate so he can support her from time to time; she hands him her letters before she dies, always knows where Bastião hides and never tells (Interlude); reads of the lottery and goes to Bastião; takes Bastião down the river as his captured runaway past the capitães do mato who know him, then leaves him with a pass naming Tonico as owner, Luzia's bundle of letters, and his own, to be read first: it breaks the news of her death, then apologises for never helping, he was a boy and afraid; he cannot leave the estate; he signs as the milk brother, knowing he was never a coffee brother (Interlude; read in ch. 13). Knows his insulting letter sent Ezequiel to burn the hideout and kill Kalunga; understands he should have killed Ezequiel and regrets offering him a peaceful way out: his one failure that can never be repaired (Interlude, decided Nov 2, 2026). Never goes to Liberdade; after the letter there is no news of him again (decided Oct 30, 2026).
      - Goes to Bastião's hiding place unarmed and alone; never part of the Kalunga clan (decided Nov 2, 2026); catches the same-day steamer with Bastião and gives him the pass, the letters and money for the journey (Interlude).
