# Firmino

- Speech voice: none of their own; the group voice [`BASE-SPEECH-BLACK-SOLDIERS`](../voices/BASE-SPEECH-BLACK-SOLDIERS.md)
- Part of the Character Bible; the index, the stages and the Continuity record are in `characters/character-bible.md`.

  - **Firmino**: war comrade freed by the war; a few short encounters in the war (ch. 4–9); in Corumbá they help each other and sometimes work together (ch. 10); his most loyal, wage-paid worker and right hand; dies slowly of an old war wound in the 1880s; his death leaves the farm without its old hands (ch. 14, 15, 17). Real aspect: freed veterans looking for work and land.
