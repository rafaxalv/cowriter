# BASE-LETTERS-DESPERATE: Letters from women in desperate conditions

  - **Type**: Base voice (not used directly in the text)
  - **Version**: 0.1 (draft) | **Status**: draft | **Created**: 2026-10-05

## Who and when

Starting point for the letters of female characters in desperate conditions. Each such character gets her own letter voice derived from this base (ID pattern: CHAR--LETTERS) and adjusted to her literacy.

## Tags

  - Narrative person: 1st
  - Addressed to: a specific person inside the story (set per character)
  - Form: letter (spoken or dictated in character, if she cannot write)
  - Literacy: set per character (to be defined in each derived voice)

## Reference authors

  - Svetlana Alexievich, 100% (decided by Rafael): broken, oral, raw testimony; small physical memories; contradictions left standing.

## Target profile

To be defined with the voice-generator skill. Starting point: Alexievich's scores in the 00 Author Library, adjusted per character.

## Literacy reminder

In the 1860s most enslaved and poor women in Brazil could not write. For each character decide: does she write herself, or is the letter dictated to someone (and does the scribe's hand show)?
