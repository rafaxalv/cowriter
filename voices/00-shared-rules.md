# 00 Shared Rules

Version 0.1 (draft), 2026-10-05. Rules every voice follows. Built from Rafael's decisions so far; to be confirmed and expanded.

## Tone

1.  No morals are pushed on the reader. The narrator never states who was right or wrong.
2.  No pure heroes or villains, Black or white. Show contradictions and the reasons behind decisions instead of judging them.
3.  Events are driven largely by luck and chance, bringing both fortune and misery.
4.  Core elements to play with: luck, freedom, violence, money. Freedom is questioned: life after freedom is not substantially better.

## History

1.  History is blended into the fiction, with no factual step-outs from the story (no lectures, no explanatory asides from outside the story).
2.  Low-reliability material (folk legends, hearsay, lies) is welcome, but only when a character says it, never stated by the narrator as fact.

## Names

1.  The great-great-grandfather is not named. The Spanish great-great-grandmother is not named.

## Bilingual

1.  Every passage exists in English and Brazilian Portuguese, side by side, saying the same thing.
2.  When Rafael's mom writes a part in Portuguese, the English is updated to match.
